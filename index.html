Vision & Positioning

This is not a standard peptide reseller. We are building a European biohacking brand grounded in transparent third-party testing, scientific rigor, and local/regional trust. The wedge is high-purity research peptides with public COAs. The moat will be deep educational content + systems thinking.

UNFAIR ADVANTAGE
You build systems for a living. This combines automation + supply chain + content. Most competitors fail at one or more of these.
BRAND POSITIONING
Different from US "bro science" shops and shady Alibaba resellers. European, clinical, transparent.
EXPANSION PATH
Peptides → Red light therapy → PEMF, grounding, sauna → full health optimization stack.
