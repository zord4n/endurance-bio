SI enforcement moderate, HR lenient (BPC-157 discovered at Univ of Zagreb).

EU DIRECTIVE 2001/83/EC

Defines medicinal products by "presentation" (what you claim) and by "function" (pharmacological effects). Most active peptides could qualify by function. The "Research Use Only" label is accepted only for genuine lab use. Selling with syringes or injection guides = trigger for enforcement. No medical claims — EVER.
